Extension settings

Snappy Pageload

Warm up the pages you can see.

01

Preloading

Choose what Snappy Pageload prepares as you browse.

Turn off to stop preloading and image lazy loading on every site. The toolbar rocket turns grey while it is off.

Preloads eligible same-origin links as they scroll into view, up to 30 per page you open. Hovering over or touching a link always preloads it.

For each visible link, also warm up the scripts, stylesheets, fonts, and a few images found in the destination page. Resources loaded later by scripts may be missed. In Safari, visible links are only preloaded when this is on.

Turns on Firefox's link prefetching, DNS prefetching and network prediction, even if a privacy setup such as arkenfox or LibreWolf turned them off. Turn off to restore your own setting.

Lets images load as they come into view. This can alter image loading on the current page.

02

Sites to skip

Snappy Pageload stays off on these sites.

Enter comma-separated hosts, such as www.example.com or *.example.org to include all subdomains.
