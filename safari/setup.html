Snappy Pageload for Safari

Enable Snappy Pageload in Safari Settings → Extensions and allow access to the websites where you want to use it. Reload existing tabs.

Click the rocket to open the settings, where you can turn Snappy Pageload off or manage visible-link preloading, page assets, image lazy loading, and excluded sites.

This local Safari build reads the destination pages of links on the same site and protocol and warms the resources their markup and stylesheets declare, without running their scripts. Resources requested only after scripts run may be missed. Faster navigation depends on Safari reusing its cache and is not guaranteed.

Visible links only do anything in Safari when Include page assets is on. Hover preloading does nothing in Safari, which does not support rel=prefetch.

Visible-link preloading and page assets are on by default. Up to 30 destination pages and 100 assets (20 per destination) may be requested for each page you open, including pages you never open. Settings stay on this Mac.
