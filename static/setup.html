Snappy Pageload is installed

Preload the pages you can see

Snappy Pageload prepares a link on the same site and protocol when you rest the pointer on it, touch it or click it. It also prepares eligible links as they scroll into view, along with the scripts, stylesheets, fonts, and a few images declared in the destination page and its stylesheets. Destination scripts are not executed, so resources loaded only at runtime may be missed.

Visible-link preloading and page assets are on by default. They can download pages you never open: each page you open is limited to 30 destinations and 100 assets, with up to 20 assets per destination.

Preloading uses the browser's <link rel="prefetch">. If nothing is preloaded in Firefox, open about:config and check that network.prefetch-next is true. It is the default, but privacy-hardened setups (arkenfox, LibreWolf) and some policies turn it off.

Click the rocket in the toolbar to open the settings, where you can change these options, exclude a site or turn Snappy Pageload off. Image lazy loading remains optional. Reload open tabs after changing settings. Faster navigation depends on the website's cache headers and your browser reusing the early downloads.
